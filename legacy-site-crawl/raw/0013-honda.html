Yamaha Generators
  
 
Yamaha EF1000

The EF1000 is the most popular Yamaha generator — and no wonder. It's extremely quiet and offers 120V AC and 12V DC — that's why it's a real hit with all types of outdoor adventurers. The EF1000 is small, easy to carry and can run a microwave, washing machine or small refrigerator. This quiet generator can run for over six hours without refueling. It delivers 1000 watts of maximum AC output with outstanding fuel efficiency. Built-in handle makes carrying a snap. Includes 12-volt DC output with protector for charging 12-volt batteries at 10-amp charge rate (charge cables included).

Yamaha EF4600DE

The EF4600DE is a true workhorse — and it represents a genuine value. This is the lowest-priced full-feature generator that is capable of running multiple appliances all at once. In addition, it can be converted to run on propane or LP gas — that makes it appealing to cabin owners. The electric-starting EF4600DE (with automatic choke and recoil backup) uses a 301cc, 4-stroke, OHV engine to deliver full-time dual-voltage (120/240V) power for over 10 hours of continuous running on a tank of gas. Fuel-saving automatic idle control lowers engine speed when generator is at no load.
 
 
 
 
Yamaha EF6600DE

When your customer wants a lot of power, then the EF6600DE is a logical choice. It's capable of running most homes in an emergency situation... and it can be converted to run on propane or LP gas. The EF6600DE has the same exterior dimensions as the EF4600DE, yet it delivers 2,000 watts more power. The EF6600DE uses a 357cc, 4-stroke, OHV engine to produce serious, full-time dual-voltage power to take on heavy-load tasks like air conditioners and refrigerators. This generator provides 8.3 hours of continuous operation without refueling. And its electric starter ensures hassle-free starting.

Yamaha EF12000DE

With 50 amps and 120/240 dual voltage output, there's not much the EF12000DE can't handle during a power outage. The EF12000DE is our highest output gasoline-powered 120/240-volt output generator — and it can be converted to run on propane or LP gas. It uses a twin-cylinder 653cc, 4-stroke, OHV engine to deliver 12,000 watts maximum AC output with outstanding fuel efficiency. It provides over 10 hours of continuous, super-quiet operation without refueling to power up super-heavy-load equipment like air compressors, table saws, and more.
