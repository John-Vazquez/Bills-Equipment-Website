4-1/2 in. Sander/Grinder (6140-6)

The 6140-6 4-1/2 inch 5.5 amp grinder is ideal for getting into tight spaces where other grinders can not. Its low profile and light weight make it perfect for those lighter duty sanding and grinding projects. The tool is double insulated (two prong cord) and incorporates the industry standard 5/8-11 threaded arbor. Like its heavy duty big brothers, it also has spiral bevel gears for optimum transmission of torque to the work piece. The comfortable body grip style is easy to hold onto, and in conjunction with the supplied side handle, allows for maximum operator control. The side handle can be attached in three locations. The wheel guard can be moved around the spindle (without the use of tools) for greatest operator protection. Sold as a grinder with guard, this tool can be used as a sander when a 4-1/2 inch backing pad and disc are attached.

11 Amp Orbital Action Super Sawzall
(6521-21)

Orbital action has been added to the heavy duty 11 amp Super Sawzall® Reciprocating Saw for even more aggressive cutting applications in wood. The orbital action selector allows the user to set the degree of orbital action for faster cutting in thick materials. This tool incorporates the patented gear protection system which absorbs high impact forces caused by sudden blade lock-up thereby extending the life of the tool even under the most extreme cutting conditions. The patented counter balance mechanism makes the Super Sawzall the smoothest operating, most vibration free saw in the world. Trigger speed control allows the operator to match the speed of the tool to the application. The new heavy duty steel Quik-Lok™ blade clamp system enables the user to change blades fast and easy without the use of a hex wrench.

 
   
 

Milwaukee 10 Amp Sawzall® Reciprocating Saw w/Case (6509-21)

Milwaukee's 10 amp Sawzall® Reciprocating Saw is the most durable reciprocating saw on the market, and at only 7 pounds, it offers the best power to weight ratio in its class. The saw offers a 0-2800 strokes per minute variable speed trigger with 3/4 in. stroke length and a Quik-Lok™ blade clamp for fast, tool-less blade changes. The rubber boot provides superior gripping, and the adjustable shoe provides longer blade life and more cut control. The blades can even be clamped upside down for special cutting needs.
