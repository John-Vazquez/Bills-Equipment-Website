Paving Breakers
  
 
The Sullair breakers have eliminated most high-wear parts. Fewer parts mean less wear, less maintenance, and less downtime.
A leak-proof air cushion forms at the end of the piston stroke and stops the piston from direct impact when the tool is not breaking. The elimination of side rods, nuts and springs makes Sullair breakers sleeker and more comfortable to operate.

Standardization of design permits a high degree of interchangeability of parts between Sullair models.

The direct impact piston design, non-corrosive automatic valve and one-piece housing dramatically reduce air consumption. You can operate more tools from a single compressor. For example, a 125 cfm compressor will operate two MPB 90 breakers from one compressor.

Rock Drills
  
 
The Sullair drills are designed so that the tool automatically stops operating when the operator releases the throttle lever.

The single throttle lever controls all tool functions from off, to blowing only, to progressive drilling speeds.

The unique design of the Sullair drills eliminates many high wear parts. This means less loss of power due to wear, less downtime to replace worn parts and lower overall maintenance costs.

With only two parts moving during operation, a non-corrosive automatic valve, less radial piston movement and high tech alloys, the Sullair drills do not require special rock drill oil. Normal oil carry-over from the compressor, combined with moisture in the air, will usually provide sufficient lubrication under normal operating conditions.

Chipping Hammers
  
 
The unique four bolt backhead design reduces handle breakage. The common problem of a threaded handle-to-barrel connection coming loose and damaging the handle and internal parts has been eliminated.

The strong “D” configuration is large enough to provide a comfortable, sure grip, even for a gloved hand.

The Sullair chipping hammer is available in 2", 3" and 4" strokes with either .680 round or .580 hex bushings.

Oval collar retainers are included as standard. Optional retainers available are universal ball type, round collar, or quick change segment retainers.

Two air inlet bushings are furnished: 3/8" NPT and 7/8-24, internal thread. The bushings provide for versatile hose connections while protecting the handle inlet threads.
