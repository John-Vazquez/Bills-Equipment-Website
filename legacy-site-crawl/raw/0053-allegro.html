8" Plastic COM-PAX-IAL Blower

This lightweight, corrosion, UV and chemical resistant polyethylene housing offers a superior design with durable construction. The carry handle is molded into the blower. This compact and super quiet unit features a polypropylene nine blade fan, a steel/powder coated grill. The bottom enclosure ensures safe and convenient storage of electrical components. Designed to work separately with canister and ducting. The quick-connect clipping system allows workers to attach canister, tools-free, to input side for powerful extraction or output side for ventilation.
