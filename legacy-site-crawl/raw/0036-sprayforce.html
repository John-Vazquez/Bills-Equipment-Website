  Twister

The Twister Plus multi-purpose sprayer is designed for patching, touch-up work and small jobs. It is the perfect second machine. Its portable size and electric motor make it ideal for interior and high rise applications. Plugs into any standard 115 Volt, 20 amp. outlet. The Twister features manually operated variable pump speed adjustment from .33 to 2.5 G.P.M. (based on water). This allows for precise control for various pumping requirements. Twister Grouter has variable pump speed and No air. It is used for grouting tile or log home chinking. It will pump and spray drywall texture, acoustic, EIFS, grout, sand/cement slurries, fireproofing and many other highly viscous and abrasive materials.

NOTE: The Twister is limited on air for atomization requirements for some materials. For example, the Twister will only spray a heavy knock-down drywwall texture. For a finer spray, air from another source must be used.

 
   
 

The Super Texan

The Super Texan is designed for small jobs. It is equipped with a 2L3 Rotor-Stator Pump that can pump 5 Gallons per minute (based on water) The polegun is air controlled and the engine is a rope start. The Super Texan is versatile and can pump drywall texture, sand finish, fireproofing, acoustic, finish coat for traditional plaster and many other highly viscous and abrasive materials. However, the lack of speed control limits its use in many applications. An optional by-pass valve is available for use when pumping drywall texture only.
